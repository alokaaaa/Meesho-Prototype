1 · Pre-delivery confirm: availability check + in-app reschedule

Customer not reachable · 30% of RTO−0.60 pp

Sent when the parcel reaches the last-mile hub (LMDC), only to orders where any 2 of 5 flags fire. One WhatsApp asks if the buyer is available in the planned slot. She can say yes, or open the Meesho app to reschedule. Replies close at 21:00, and the 05:00 manifest is built by slot.

2 · DIGIPIN captured at checkout, plus geocode routing

Address unclear / wrong hub · 27% of RTO−2.20 pp

At checkout the buyer says who the order is for. Self (72%): one draggable map pin, no login, DIGIPIN written to the order. Someone else (15.4%): the recipient gets a request and drops their own pin. It's one-way, so the buyer only sees "Address confirmed". No response in 12 hours (12.6%): the order falls back to the saved address. At destination sort, the DIGIPIN routes the parcel to the right hub.

3 · CoD risk score: evidence, not a ratio

CoD refusal · 25% of RTO−0.69 pp

s = (refusals + α) ÷ (total CoD orders + k), with α = 1.06, k = 20 and m = 5.3%. It's never applied below 5 real CoD orders. Damaged or wrong items, and refusals the buyer denies, never count. Controls come in four graduated tiers, and there is always a way back.

4 · DIGIPIN check at "attempt failed"

No real attempt · 13% of RTO−0.92 pp

When a rider taps any failure code, their location becomes a DIGIPIN and is compared with the delivery DIGIPIN. The limit is 150 m (dense urban), 200 m (semi-urban) or 250 m (rural), floored at 2× the GPS error. Inside the limit the failure is accepted; outside, the parcel stays live. There's no penalty either way. Three guards close the relabelling trap.

5 · Kirana holding nodes

Customer not reachable · Red lane only−0.65 pp

Red-lane parcels are pulled out at the last-mile hub and dropped together at a kirana (6–8 parcels, ₹8 each). The shop holds them for up to 72 hours, and the buyer walks 300–400 m to collect with an OTP and pay. It's deployed only where base RTO beats the 34.2% break-even.

6 · Address change, bounded by Voronoi cells

Address unclear / wrong hub−0.80 pp

Only in the app, with no message prompting it. The buyer can move the delivery only into a hub cell that shares an edge with the current one, so the re-route is a lateral move with a known cost. The guardrails are adjacent cell only, one change, a 9 pm cut-off, and both-or-neither for multi-parcel stops.
